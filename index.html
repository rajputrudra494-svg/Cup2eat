Why we started

A paper cup works for exactly one chai. Then it becomes someone else’s problem — most of them are lined with plastic, so they don’t really go away, they just go somewhere else. We thought the cup deserved a better ending. So we made one you can eat.

How it works

One cup,
three acts.

  1. 01

    Pour.

    Hot chai, filter coffee, cold coffee, a shake. It’s baked to be leak-proof, so whatever you pour stays exactly where it should.

  2. 02

    Sip.

    Drink it the way you always do. The only difference is the rim — chocolate, vanilla or elaichi, right under your lip. (Chocolate with coffee is a dangerous habit. Just saying.)

  3. 03

    Eat.

    Then bite in. Crunchy, a little sweet, 100% edible. Nothing goes in the bin, because there’s nothing left to put in it.

Build your cup

Three flavours.
Pick a favourite.

Vanilla

The golden, biscuity one. Gentle enough to sit under masala chai without fighting it, and the one most people finish first.

Pairs with masala chai · cold coffee · shakes

Chocolate

Dark, cocoa-rich and built for coffee. We also do a chocolate-coated version for ice cream — the bit at the bottom is the best bit.

Pairs with espresso · latte · ice cream

Elaichi

Green cardamom, the way your nani put it in everything. Pour plain chai in this one and it tastes like someone made it for you.

Pairs with cutting chai · kadak chai · kheer

Chocolate rim

Our newest one. Chocolate only on the top lip, right where your mouth goes, so every sip gets a little extra — without coating the whole cup.

Pairs with cappuccino · hot chocolate · cold coffee

Our story

Started by a student
with a very simple question.

Cup2Eat is Varun Khera’s idea, and it started while he was still doing his BBA at IPEM, Ghaziabad. The question was simple enough to be annoying: why should the cup be the one thing you throw away?

He took the idea to business-plan competitions before he took it to customers — and won first place at the Planpreneur competition at SPECTRA 2025. Since then it’s been stalls at college fests, corporate events and celebrations (twenty-plus certificates and counting), and boxes leaving Ghaziabad for every corner of the country.

It’s still a small team. Every order is packed by hand, and every message on WhatsApp gets answered by a person.

Questions

The ones
everyone asks.

(usually while holding one)

Is the whole cup actually edible?

Yes. It’s a baked cup — rim, wall and base — and it’s 100% edible, pure veg and vegan. Finish your drink, then finish the cup.

Will it leak with hot chai?

It’s made to be leak-proof for both hot and cold drinks. Like anything baked, it’s at its crunchiest when you drink while it’s hot — which, with chai, has never been the problem.

What flavours and sizes do you have?

The 100 ml hot cup comes in Chocolate, Vanilla and Elaichi. There’s a 150 ml cup for cold coffee and shakes, a fully chocolate-coated 100 ml cup, and our new chocolate-rim cup (chocolate only on the lip).

What does it cost?

Regular 100 ml: ₹3,999 for 396 cups on this month’s offer (minimum 5 boxes). 150 ml cold: ₹6,500 for 432 or ₹3,300 for 125. Chocolate-coated: ₹4,499 for 200. Chocolate rim: ₹5,500 for 396. Delivery is included anywhere in India — message us and we’ll confirm the latest price and stock.

What’s it made of?

Refined wheat flour (maida), wheat flour, pulses, corn flour, dextrose sugar, salt, emulsifiers, permitted food colours (INS 102, 122, 133), acidity regulator (INS 514), vegetable oil, baking powder (E500), flavouring, preservative (E223) and spices. If you have an allergy, ask us before you order.
